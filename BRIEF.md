## The problem
- people in our school stuggle with finding the classroom at the first week becaouse of few clear sign and more new student unfamiliar with the school building.
- Impact area: Something else
- Why it matters (impact): New student may find the classrorom quickly and feel less stressed and confused at the started of the school.They can arrive the classroom on time and feel more welcome in the school
- Evidence it’s real: 83.78% of surveyed people had wayfinding difficulties inside a teaching building, and 96.78% of those lost people struggled most with finding classrooms, often because of unclear signage.
- What people told me: Sunny:"It happend on my first day of school.I had to stop teachers to ask where my classroom is.Big sign of the Hallway may help a lot.
## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by giving class room directions,which work beter than asking teachers because new students get instant guidance
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Our school has printed maps and signs, but they do not give step-by-step directions. My AI gives instant simple directions for lost new students.
- Safety plan: Problem: The AI gives wrong directions to a classroom.
Fix: It only uses the official school map information stored in the system.
## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can type a room number and get step-by-step walking directions.
2. The user can pick their starting spot from a list of school landmarks.
3. The user can see the room's floor and building on a simple school map.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
## Maybe later (not yet)

## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user types a book description and presses Search.
- Its job: HELP students
- Who it talks to and how to sound: middle school student casual tone
- Facts it must use: Our school library book collection list, library opening hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM, closed on weekends and school holidays.
- Rules it must follow: never share students personal information
- How to answer: keep answer brief
